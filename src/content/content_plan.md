# IAEVA Content Strategy Plan: Outrank thethingapp.com

## Executive Summary

After analyzing thethingapp.com's website and SEO strategy, we've developed a comprehensive content plan to help IAEVA outrank them in search results. TheThing focuses on AI-powered customer service and sales automation, targeting primarily Spanish-speaking markets. To surpass them, we'll leverage IAEVA's unique positioning as a healthcare-focused AI assistant while implementing advanced SEO techniques and creating superior content that truly addresses user needs.
